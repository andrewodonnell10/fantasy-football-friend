# League Rules & Settings

## Keeper League Rules/Restrictions

- Up to TWO players can be kept on your roster the following year at a Keeper Draft Value (KDV) of the round ahead of where they were drafted.
  - Example: if you drafted a player in the 7th round, you can keep them as your 6th round pick the following draft.
- Keeper(s) must be identified, communicated to, and verified by the commissioner at least ONE WEEK BEFORE THE DRAFT.
  - Note: this is important because the commissioner needs to set things up ahead of time in Yahoo to make the draft go smoothly.
- Dropping a player at any point in the season makes them NOT Keeper Eligible.
- Players drafted in the First Round are NOT Keeper Eligible.
- Players picked up from the waiver wire in season are NOT Keeper Eligible.
- Trading a player makes them NOT Keeper Eligible for the new team manager.
- A player may be eligible as Keeper on the same team TWICE (consecutively). The KDV for the first time is as stated above, and the second time will be at their prior season Average Draft Position (ADP) according to Yahoo.
  - Note: If ADP is in the first round, they are NOT Keeper Eligible.
  - Example: if you first drafted a player in the 7th round, you can keep them as your 6th round pick the following draft. If their Yahoo ADP for that draft is a 3rd round pick, you can keep them a second time (in the subsequent season) as your 3rd round pick, since that was their prior year ADP.

When selecting keepers (or deciding not to), make sure they follow the rules/restrictions above, and at minimum, that you originally drafted them. Contact the Commissioner with any questions on this ruleset.
